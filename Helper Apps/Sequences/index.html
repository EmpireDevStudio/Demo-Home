The Five Sequence Elements

Slots I through V require a descriptive stage title, an authentic answer, and an authentic decoy.

I

Slot 1: Foundation

Typically Base Spirit, Primary Distillate, or Initial Glass Prep
II

Slot 2: Sweetener & Modifiers

Syrup, liqueur, cordial, or modifier
III

Slot 3: Acid & Seasoning

Aromatic bitters, citrus juice, or saline
IV

Slot 4: Method & Agitation

Chilling method, dilution technique, or aeration
V

Slot 5: Vessel & Garnish Finish

Glassware service, aromatic peel expression, or crown
