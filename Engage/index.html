01 / Before and After

Stop repeating yourself. Put knowledge in one place.

Good training fails when notes and rules are hard to find. Compare a scattered setup with one central hub.

Before: The Problem
  • Files spread across folders, chats, and paper notes
  • Staff often asking managers for basic answers
  • Different versions of training instructions
  • Rules and steps that are hard to find during a shift
  • Managers losing time repeating the same advice
After: Central Training Hub
  • One central place for every training guide
  • Staff open what they need on their own devices
  • Clear, matching guides, checklists, and procedures
  • Daily learning prompts to keep knowledge fresh
  • Less need for managers to repeat the basics
