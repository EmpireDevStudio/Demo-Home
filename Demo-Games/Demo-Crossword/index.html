        How to Play

        01

        Ledger Objective

        Fill every vacant cardstock cell by solving the curated vocabulary and thematic cues. Sunken dark cells mark terminal boundaries.

        02

        Orientation & Track Focus

        Select any cell to inspect its active clue. Tap the active cell again or click the DIR button to toggle between Across and Down word tracks.

        03

        Tactile Input

        Use your physical keyboard or the onscreen virtual keys. The input cursor advances automatically to the next letter. Press Backspace to erase and step back.

        04

        Three Daily Tiers

        Each release features Mini (5×5 swift solve), Midi (patterned mid-tier), and Main (full challenge). All solves are recorded in the Vault.
