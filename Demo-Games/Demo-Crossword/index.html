        HOUSE RULES & GUIDE

        How to Play

        01

        Objective

        Fill every open cardstock cell of the ledger grid by solving the curated clues provided. Dark cells mark word boundaries.

        02

        Navigation & Orientation

        Select any cell to inspect its active clue. Tap the active cell again or press the DIR button to toggle between Across and Down word tracks.

        03

        Typing & Corrections

        Type on your physical keyboard or use the tactile screen keys. The cursor advances automatically to the next letter. Press Backspace to erase and step back.

        04

        Daily Releases & Tiers

        Each day, a new release rotates across three tiers: Mini (5×5 fast solve), Midi (shaped grid), and Main (full edition). Past puzzles remain accessible in the Vault.
