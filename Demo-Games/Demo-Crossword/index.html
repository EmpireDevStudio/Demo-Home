      House Rules & Guide

      How to Play

      01

      Objective

      Fill every open cell of the ledger grid by solving the mixology and vocabulary clues provided. Dark obsidian cells delineate word boundaries.

      02

      Navigation & Orientation

      Select any cell to inspect its active clue. Tap the active cell again or use the DIR button to toggle between Across and Down word tracks.

      03

      Typing & Corrections

      Type on your physical keyboard or use the tactile screen keys. The cursor advances automatically to the next letter. Use Backspace to erase and step back.

      04

      Daily Releases & Tiers

      Each day, a new ledger release unlocks across three tiers: Mini (5×5 fast solve), Midi (shaped grid), and Main (full edition). Past puzzles remain accessible in the Vault.
