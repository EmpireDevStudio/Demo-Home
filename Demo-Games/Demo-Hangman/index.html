How to Play

  1. Objective: Uncover the secret word or phrase by selecting letters one at a time before the gallows completes.
  2. Scaffold Strikes: Each incorrect guess tallies 1 strike onto the gallows apparatus.
  3. The Limit: Accumulate 6 strikes and the gallows claims the puzzle.
  4. Category & Hints: The category is always visible. Tap 💡 Hint to reveal or conceal the mixologist’s clue.
  5. Daily Challenge: A fresh challenge unlocks each day with the turn of your local calendar date.
  6. The Vault: Explore and solve any puzzle from the complete lounge cellar library at any time.
