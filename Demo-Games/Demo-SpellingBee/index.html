EDITORIAL RULES & GUIDE

How to Play

Core Objective

Construct valid English vocabulary words from the 7 letters in the honeycomb. Always include the center letter in every single entry.

Hive Directives

  • Words must be at least 4 letters long.
  • Every word must contain the center letter at least once.
  • Letters may be used multiple times.
  • Proper nouns, slang, obscenities, and hyphenated terms are strictly excluded.
