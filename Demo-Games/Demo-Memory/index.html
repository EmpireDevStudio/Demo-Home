How to Play

Objective

Test your visual recall by uncovering all 8 matching pairs on the 4×4 lounge grid in the fewest turns possible.

Lounge Etiquette

  • Tactile Reveal: Select any smoked tile to rotate it and inspect its custom botanical symbol.
  • Seek the Mate: Turn a second tile over. If their emblems match, they illuminate and lock in liquid gold.
  • Mismatches: Unmatched tiles will pause briefly before turning back face down. Memorize their marks.
  • Daily Cellar: A brand new curated puzzle unlocks every evening at midnight local time.
