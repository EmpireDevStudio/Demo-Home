House Rules

How to Play

Objective

Uncover all 8 matching botanical ingredient pairs on the 4×4 tactile grid in the fewest turns possible.

Tactile Mechanics

  • Reveal: Select any paper card to rotate it and inspect its botanical mark.
  • Find the Mate: Select a companion card. Matching pairs illuminate in sage green and lock into place.
  • Mismatches: Non-matching pairs return face down after a brief inspection interval. Memorize their positions.
  • Daily Release: A newly curated botanical tasting unlocks every evening at midnight local time.

Performance Standards

  • ★★★ Masterful: 12 turns or fewer
  • ★★☆ Distinguished: 13–18 turns
  • ★☆☆ Cleared: 19+ turns
