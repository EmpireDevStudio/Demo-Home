CANONICAL PROTOCOL

How to Play Sequence

  1. Analyse the Mandate: Read the formulation clue. Slots I through V follow an exact chronological, ingredient balance, or structural preparation order.
  2. Assigning Ingredients: Select any slot (I to V) to activate it, then tap an ingredient from the pool. Tapping an active, filled slot returns the ingredient to the pool.
  3. Four Verification Attempts: You receive 4 attempts. Submitting verifies all 5 slots. Correct items lock permanently with a gold seal (✦). Decoys and misplaced items return to the pool.
  4. Tasting Notes Revelation: After 2 failed attempts, a bespoke sensory tasting note is uncovered to steer your mixology logic.
